University Financial Aid Verification Policy and Procedure
Academic Year 2026-2027

Introduction
The Office of Financial Aid is required by federal regulations to verify the information submitted on the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). Students selected for verification must complete all required steps before financial aid awards can be disbursed.

Step 1: Check Your Student Portal
Students must log into their University Student Portal within 7 business days of receiving notification to view outstanding verification requirements listed under the Financial Aid To-Do list.

Step 2: Complete the Verification Worksheet
The student and parents (if dependent) must download, complete, and sign the Dependent or Independent Verification Worksheet. All sections must be filled out completely.

Step 3: Submit Federal Tax Return Transcripts
Provide an official IRS Tax Return Transcript or signed copy of the 2024 IRS Form 1040 with all applicable W-2 statements and 1099 forms to the Financial Aid Office.

Step 4: Resolve Conflicting Information
If discrepancies arise between the FAFSA and submitted documentation, students must meet with a Financial Aid Counselor within 10 business days to provide clarification or updated forms.

Step 5: Final Review and Aid Disbursement
Once all documentation is submitted and verified by the counselor, the Financial Aid Office will finalize the student's aid package and post awards to the student account within two to three weeks.
