About State University - Mission, Vision, and Core Values

Our History
Founded in 1888, State University has been dedicated to excellence in teaching, research, and public service for over a century. Located in the heart of the Pacific Northwest, our campus spans 300 acres of lush pine forests and modern architectural facilities.

Mission Statement
State University transforms lives through high-impact learning experiences, groundbreaking scholarly discovery, and an enduring commitment to community engagement and global citizenship.

Core Values
- Integrity: We uphold the highest ethical standards in all personal and professional interactions.
- Innovation: We encourage bold thinking and pioneering methodologies across all academic disciplines.
- Collaboration: We cultivate multidisciplinary partnerships that address pressing regional and planetary challenges.
- Inclusivity: We celebrate diverse backgrounds, experiences, and worldviews as essential to educational excellence.

Campus Leadership
The University is governed by the Board of Trustees in consultation with the Faculty Senate and Student Government Association.
